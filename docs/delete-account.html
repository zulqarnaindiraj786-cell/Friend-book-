Account & Personal Data Deletion — friendbook

App: friendbook (com.friendbook.social)

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the friendbook app and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Menu (☰) → Settings & Privacy.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account & Personal Data and tap Delete forever.
  4. Your Firebase Authentication account, profile document, posts, reels, stories, and comments are permanently deleted immediately.

Option 2: Web / Email Account Deletion Request (Without Opening the App)

If you have uninstalled the app and wish to permanently delete your friendbook account and all associated data, click below to send a deletion request from your registered email address:

Request Permanent Account Deletion via Email

What Data Is Deleted & Retention Policy

  • Deleted Permanently: Firebase Auth credentials, user profile (`users/{uid}`), posts, reels, stories, comments, and saved preferences (processed immediately in-app, or within 7 days for email requests).
  • Retained Only Where Legally Required: Anonymized safety/abuse audit logs if an account was flagged for severe legal/CSAE violations.
