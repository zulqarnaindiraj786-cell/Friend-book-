Privacy Policy — friendbook

App Name: friendbook  |  Package: com.friendbook.social  |  Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Display name, email address, and optional profile details provided when you register via Firebase Authentication.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Posts, comments, stories, reels, photos, videos, and optional voice notes (`RECORD_AUDIO` permission used only when you actively tap the microphone button in Messenger).
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers: Google AdMob SDK (`ca-app-pub-3387480177023669~1513026594`) may use the Android Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`) to serve Sponsored Native Ads in accordance with your Google UMP / GDPR consent choices.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

  • All network traffic is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS (`usesCleartextTraffic="false"`).
  • Account, social graph, and chat records are stored securely on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore & Realtime Database). Media files are hosted over HTTPS on Cloudinary / Firebase Storage.
  • On-device automated content safety screening inspects uploaded media locally to prevent explicit nudity, graphic violence, and CSAE violations.
  • We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. User Controls, GDPR Consent & Account Deletion

  • Ad Privacy (GDPR / UMP): Review or withdraw ad personalization consent anytime inside the app at Settings → Ad Privacy & GDPR Consent (UMP).
  • Block & Report: Block any user or report any post, reel, story, or message in 1 tap.
  • Data Export & Permanent Deletion: Delete your account and personal data immediately in-app via Settings → Delete Account & Personal Data, or via our web deletion page at Account Deletion Request.

4. Child Safety

friendbook is intended for users aged 13 and older and enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Read our published Child Safety Standards.
