Child Safety Standards & Zero-Tolerance CSAE Policy

App: friendbook (com.friendbook.social)  |  Effective: October 2026

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy Against CSAE & CSAM

friendbook maintains an absolute, unconditional zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), grooming, minor endangerment, or any sexualized content involving minors.

2. Automated Prevention & Content Moderation

  • Pre-Upload & Feed Safety Screening: friendbook runs automated text and visual media safety detection across posts, reels, stories, and chat uploads to block and automatically delete prohibited explicit or abusive content.
  • Mandatory User Agreement: Every user must confirm they are 13+ and accept friendbook's Zero-Tolerance UGC & Child Safety Standards before creating an account.
  • 1-Tap In-App Reporting: Users can report suspected CSAE or harmful content directly from any Post, Reel, Story, Profile, Chat, or via Settings → Child Safety Standards (Zero-Tolerance CSAE) → Report Child Safety Concern Immediately.

3. Enforcement & Law Enforcement Reporting

  • Any content violating Child Safety Standards is immediately deleted from Cloud Firestore and media storage.
  • Offending accounts are permanently terminated and banned without warning.
  • Confirmed CSAM/CSAE incidents are preserved as required by applicable law and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities.
